How it works

From satellite to farmer in 4 simple steps.

1

Register

Farmer shares location, boundary, crop and sowing date by SMS or WhatsApp.

2

Fetch data

Get satellite and weather data for the farm boundary.

3

Analyse

Process cloud mask, NDVI/NDWI and align with crop stage.

4

Detect & alert

Compare with the expected curve and send an alert if a risk threshold is crossed.
